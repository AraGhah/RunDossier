# Preframe — Warm Pilot Outreach: Message Pack

**Goal of this step:** hand-send 10 personalized DMs to warm-ish contacts in B2B
sales/outbound, offering a free research pilot. These are your first real users —
they validate the product and generate the case studies that make cold outbound
convert later. This is **manual** outreach only. Do **not** route these through
Smartlead — your cold inboxes are still warming.

---

## The offer (one paragraph, memorize it)

> I'm testing a new prospect-research pipeline (Preframe). It takes your real
> prospects, pulls genuine signals — recent posts, podcasts, job changes, hiring,
> funding — and writes a personalized first line (subject + opener) for each one,
> QA-scored, ready to paste into your sequencer. I'll run **50–100 of your
> prospects for free**. If the lines don't beat your current opener reply rate,
> I'll rewrite the batch once free or you keep the work and owe nothing. No card,
> no commitment. The only catch: I want honest feedback and — if it's good — a
> short testimonial.

**Three pillars** (every message must contain all three):
1. **Free pilot** — 50–100 researched + personalized first lines.
2. **Reply-rate guarantee** — beats your current opener or I redo it / you owe nothing.
3. **Zero risk + honesty** — no commitment, and I'm openly testing + want feedback.

**What you need from them:** either a list of 50–100 prospects (name + LinkedIn or
company), or just their ICP and I'll source the list. That's it.

## Follow-ups (only for non-responders — keep it light)

**Bump 1 (after 3–4 days):**
> Floating this back up, {{first}} — happy to just personalize **one** of your
> prospects so you can see the quality before deciding. Want me to?

**Bump 2 (after ~7 more days, then stop):**
> Last nudge — I'll leave it here so I'm not a pest. If reply rates ever become the
> bottleneck, the free batch offer stands. Either way, rooting for you.

> Rule: maximum **two** follow-ups. These are friends-of-friends — protect the
> relationship over the conversion.

## Objection handling

- **"Is this just AI spam?"** → Opposite. It's AI doing the 20 minutes of research a
  great SDR would do per prospect if they had time — every line is grounded in a
  real, cited signal and QA-scored. I throw away the ones that read generic.
- **"What's the catch / why free?"** → No catch. It's free because I need proof it
  works on real pipelines. Feedback, and a testimonial only if it earns one.
- **"I don't have time."** → Zero lift on you. Send a list, or literally just your
  ICP, and I do the rest. You only spend time if you like the output.
- **"We already personalize."** → Then you're the perfect test — run my batch
  head-to-head against yours. If mine doesn't win, you've lost nothing.
- **"Send me a sample first."** → Happy to. Give me one prospect's name + LinkedIn
  and I'll send back the first line today.

## Guardrails
- Manual send only. **Do not** use Smartlead/cold inboxes for these — warmup is running.
- Personalize `{{warm_hook}}` for every single person. No copy-paste blasting.
- Be honest in every message that you're testing the product and want feedback.
- Two follow-ups max. Log every send in the tracker the same day.
