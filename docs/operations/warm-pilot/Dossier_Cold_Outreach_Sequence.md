# Dossier Cold Outreach Sequence (founder-led, selling Dossier)

This is for YOUR outreach to agencies, not the openers Dossier writes for clients. Built from the
combined framework: Kevin O'Leary brevity, Sam McKenna "show me you know me" plus objection handling,
Hormozi plain-text. House rule: no dashes anywhere. Use commas and periods. Plain text, no images,
no calendar link in the first email, one question CTA.

## The 6-field operating system (fill before writing any email)
1. Prospect / agency name
2. Current business signal (specific, dated)
3. Source URL for that signal
4. Inference: why that signal matters for them right now
5. Likely objection (Clay, Apollo, VAs, in-house, "another lead list")
6. Best CTA asset (default: a three-prospect source-backed sample)

If you cannot fill 2, 3, and 4, the prospect is not ready for Tier 1. Drop to a Tier 2 or 3 template.

## The framework
- Subject: a specific signal that looks ordinary to a stranger but obvious to this one prospect.
- Opening: show you noticed a real, current signal. No flattery, no "I noticed".
- Challenge: state the operational problem that signal probably creates. Describe their challenge, not your features.
- Differentiation: show why Dossier is different from their existing AI, VAs, Clay, or Apollo.
- CTA: ask permission to send a small asset. Never ask for a call first.

## Tiering (where to spend effort)
- Tier 1 (manual, high-value founder): deep research, uniquely specific subject, 90 to 130 words, one objection handled, a custom sample or mini audit.
- Tier 2 (strong-fit list): segment-based signals, reusable subject structure, 60 to 100 words, one pain point, the three-prospect sample CTA.
- Tier 3 (broad list): company and niche personalization only, under 70 words, no fake personal details, a simple question CTA.

## Objection handling (pre-empt in the email or use on reply)
| They think | What to say |
|---|---|
| "We already use Clay." | Clay sources and enriches data. Dossier turns the signal into a QA-ready delivery asset your client can verify. |
| "We already use Apollo." | Apollo is contact data and sequencing. Dossier improves the research and personalization quality before sequencing. |
| "We have VAs." | VAs can research. Dossier standardizes the quality, shows the source, and makes campaign review faster. |
| "We write first lines in-house." | The win is faster QA, visible evidence per line, and less manual research time. |
| "Sounds like another lead list." | It is not a list. It is a research-backed campaign-delivery layer: signal, source, interpretation, opener. |
| "AI personalization is generic." | The value is not AI copy. It is verified signals, clear evidence, and output a client can approve. |

## CTA ladder (never skip a step)
1. First email: ask permission to send a sample.
2. Reply received: send the three-prospect sample (signal, source, opener per row).
3. Positive reaction only: "Would it make sense to compare this against your current delivery workflow next week?"

## Inbound: respond fast (speed to contact is a top lever)

## Measure replies, not opens
Apple Mail Privacy Protection makes open rate unreliable. Track: positive replies, samples requested,
calls booked, pilots started, and pilot-to-paid conversions. A 25 percent open rate that creates five
real agency conversations beats a 50 percent open rate with none.

## Final checklist before sending
- Could this exact email go to 100 other agencies? If yes, add specificity.
- Did I explain why the signal matters, not just name it?
- Did I describe their challenge instead of Dossier's features?
- Did I handle the most likely objection?
- Did I ask for a small reply, not a meeting?
- Zero dashes. One clear next step.
