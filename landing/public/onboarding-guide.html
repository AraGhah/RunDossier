Every opener references something specific and real — a post, a launch, a hire — never generic flattery. Lines that aren't grounded in a real signal never ship.

2.What we need from you

You'll fill this in once on your onboarding form. We read it back to you for approval before sourcing anyone.

  • Your ideal customer, in plain English — role, niche, company type, and location.
  • Your offer in one sentence — what you do for them.
  • 2-3 first lines you love (optional) — so we can match your voice.
  • Target accounts (optional) — paste LinkedIn URLs or company names, or leave blank and we'll source by your ICP.

3.Reading your delivery CSV

Each delivery is one CSV, one row per prospect, ready to send. The columns you'll use most:

ColumnWhat it is
prospect_name, title, companyWho the prospect is.
emailVerified email when we could find one. Blank means no deliverable address was found for that prospect.
linkedin_urlTheir profile, for manual touches or LinkedIn outreach.
subjectA suggested subject line.
openerThe personalized first line. This is the core of each row — lead with it.
bridgeOne sentence connecting the opener to your offer.
research_signals, research_summaryThe signal the line is built on, so you can see why it works.
confidence_scoreOur research-fit score (0-100) for that prospect.

The file also includes company_domain, source_links, variant_number, and IDs for your records — ignore them unless you need them.

4.Importing to your sequencer

Smartlead
  1. Create a campaign → Import leads → upload the CSV.
  2. Map email → Email. Map opener, subject, and bridge to custom variables.
  3. In step 1 of your sequence, start with {{opener}}, then your {{bridge}} and pitch.
Instantly
  1. Leads → Import → CSV. Upload the file.
  2. Map email to the email field; map opener/subject/bridge as custom variables.
  3. Reference {{opener}} as your first line in step 1.

If your sequencer requires an email per row, filter out rows where email is blank (or enrich those separately). The opener still works for LinkedIn or manual outreach.

5.What to expect — Week 1

  • Your first batch lands by email within 3 business days of approving your ICP.
  • Review the openers, import them, and start sending.
  • See a line that misses? Reply to the delivery email and we'll recut it.

6.What to expect — Month 1

  • A new batch on your agreed cadence (weekly by default).
  • Day-14 check-in: send us 2-3 replies you got, good or bad, and we tune targeting and voice.
  • Openers sharpen over the month as we learn what lands with your audience.

7.How to request changes

Just tell us — reply to any delivery email or message us. Common requests:

  • Adjust your ICP (titles, region, company size, angle).
  • Change cadence or pause for a week.
  • Flag a specific line — we'll recut it.
